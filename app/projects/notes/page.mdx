Notes is one of my earliest published projects. Back in middle school, I was really into Android app development and wanted to make an app of my own, mostly because I thought it would be cool and fun. I'd learned the basics of Java and object-oriented programming through KTBYTE's curriculum, so I decided to try building a notes app.

Getting even the basic things to work was much harder than I expected. I spent a lot of time searching Stack Overflow, watching YouTube tutorials, and piecing together what I found. Over several weeks of working in my free time, I got the main features working: viewing, saving, and editing notes and their titles, along with deletion and a trash can.

I also added a feature that fetched announcements through Firebase. I didn't know asynchronous programming was a thing yet, so I set a timer and waited for the response to come back before trying to use it. Looking back, that part is pretty funny, but at the time I was just happy to get it working.

Once the app was ready, I packaged it into an APK. That introduced me to app signing and the process of getting an app approved for distribution. There was a whole set of things to figure out after the coding was done. I also added analytics so I could see how many people were using it.

To this day, I'm still surprised that it has around ten daily active users. I'm not sure why they'd prefer my app over their phone's built-in notes app or Google Keep, but I'm happy to take it. Something I made for fun in middle school is still useful to a few people, which feels pretty good.
